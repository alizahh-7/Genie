# Genie: privacy and data handling

Genie is used by families of people near the end of life, so privacy is built into the data layer and not added on top. This document says what Genie stores, who receives it, how long it stays, and how to remove it.

Genie is not a medical service. For questions about care, please speak with the care team.

## Principles

- Collect the minimum.
- No accounts and no passwords for families.
- Contributors see a consent note before they submit anything.
- Everything about a person can be deleted in one action.
- Data expires on its own.
- Every use of a third-party service is named here.

## What Genie stores

| Data | Where it comes from | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| A first name or nickname, birth year, region, language | The family, when they create a person | Whoever holds the person's private link |
| Favourite songs, dishes, places, films (the Taste Portrait), with a label saying where each came from | The family, the Wish Circle, and Genie's own guesses (always labelled "likely") | The family |
| Wishes suggested for the person, and suggested Wish Makers | Generated by Genie | The family |
| A contributor's first name, answers (each up to 500 characters), and a consent flag | Each Wish Circle contributor | The family |
| An optional contact for a Wish Maker request | The family | The Wish Maker, only after they accept |
| A Wish Maker's name, offer, bio, tags, city, country, languages, and private contact email | The Wish Maker, when they apply | Contact email: the Genie reviewer, and the family only after the Wish Maker accepts. The rest is public once approved |
| A Wish Maker's private link | Generated | Only the Wish Maker. Only a hash of it is stored |

Genie does not collect surnames, addresses, health information, or identification numbers. It never asks about illness, diagnosis, or medication.

## Who receives data

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Qloo** | Names of songs, artists, dishes, places, and films | To find taste links |
| **A third-party AI service** (when an AI key is configured) | Names of favourites, and the person's region and language, to read typed answers and correct spellings. If wish rewriting is switched on, the wish text as well | To understand free text |
| **The same AI service, for the comparison screen only** | The person's first name, birth year, region, language, the favourites, and the Wish Circle notes | So the general AI sees exactly what Genie saw |
| **Brevo** (email) | A recipient address and the message | Notifications to Wish Makers, and updates to a family whose contact looks like an email |
| **Supabase** | All stored data | Database hosting |
| **Render, Vercel** | Requests to the app | Hosting |

Free-tier AI services may use prompts to improve their models. This is why Genie sends only the items above and nothing else. Please leave surnames, contact details, and health details out of anything you type.

Qloo's data stays in Genie's private database and cache. It is never published, and Genie's public repository contains none of it.

## Retention and deletion

- Every person expires after 7 days by default. The family can choose 7, 30, 90, or 365 days.
- Expired data is unreadable at once, and an hourly job removes it.
- **Delete everything** removes a person and everything linked to them: the portrait, wishes, Wish Circle links and answers, matches and requests, and the Wish Book.
- A Wish Book share link is opt-in and can be switched off at any time. The shared page contains no person identifier and never shows a Wish Maker's contact details.
- A Wish Maker can withdraw their profile at any time. This removes the profile and every request linked to it.
- Two demo people are kept on purpose for the live demo. They are fictional.

## How data is protected

- Links for the Wish Circle, shared Wish Books, and person pages are long random values that cannot be guessed. There is no endpoint that lists people.
- Wish Maker links are stored only as a hash, so a database leak does not expose working links. A lost link is replaced with a new one by email, and the old one stops working.
- Row-level security is on for every table.
- Cross-origin access is limited to the app's own address.
- Requests are rate limited per address. Every response asks browsers not to cache it or send a referrer.
- All user text is escaped before it is shown, including on the printable Wish Book.
- Keys and passwords live only in environment variables and are never committed.
- Contact details are shared between a family and a Wish Maker only after both sides have agreed.
- A concern raised by a family suspends the Wish Maker at once.

## Engine cache

To stay fast and within quota, Genie caches responses from Qloo and the AI service in a private table in its own database (`engine_cache`). It holds hashed request keys and response bodies. Surnames, contact details, and health information are never sent, so they are never cached. A cached comparison answer can contain the first name that was used in it. Deleting a person does not remove cache rows.

## Known limits

- Emails already sent cannot be recalled when a family deletes their data.
- The cache is not cleared when a person is deleted.
- The hosting providers keep their own request logs under their own policies.
- Wish Makers in this build are fictional samples, clearly labelled, except any real applicant a reviewer has approved.

## Wish Maker verification plan

1. Remote wishes first. Recorded songs, messages, recipes, and stories carry the least risk and work from anywhere.
2. Every applicant is reviewed by a person before anyone is listed, and agrees to a code of conduct.
3. In-person wishes go only to makers who passed a reference check.
4. In-person wishes happen with a family member present, or through a partner hospice's volunteer coordinator.
5. No contact details are shared until both the family and the Wish Maker accept.
6. Either side can decline or withdraw at any time.
7. After each wish the family can give feedback, and any concern suspends the Wish Maker until a person reviews it.
